Usage

Do

  • Place on black or near-black grounds — that's the mark's native contrast.
  • Switch to the white mono mark on photography or busy backgrounds.
  • Switch to the black mono mark on white or paper-colored backgrounds.
  • Keep the gold gradient direction — lighter at top, deeper gold at the base.

Don't

  • Don't recolor the container icon outside the approved gold, white, or black sets.
  • Don't stretch or skew the lockup — scale proportionally only.
  • Don't run the gold logo on white — use the black mono variant instead.
  • Don't add drop shadows, strokes, or bevels not already in the source file.
